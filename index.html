Everything happens on the phone

Slovo downloads a speech model once. After that, the recorder, the recognition and the text all live on your device, with or without a connection.

Nothing leaves your device

The speech model runs on the phone. Turn on airplane mode and Slovo still turns your voice into text. There is no account to create and no server of ours in between.

Record or import

A recorder inside the app, or any file: mp3, m4a, wav, mp4. Voice memos, chat voice messages and videos arrive through the Share sheet.

Long recordings

A whole meeting, lecture or interview in one go. Recording keeps going in the background, and the text appears while you speak.

Clean up the text

An optional on-device language model adds punctuation, removes filler words and repetitions. The text reads like an article, not a stenogram.

Sync through iCloud

Transcripts and audio move between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. Or switch it off and keep everything on one device.

How it works

  1. Record or upload

    Tap the button and talk. Or hand a file over from Voice Memos, Files, Photos or any app with a Share button.

  2. Recognized on the device

    Text appears while the recording is still running. Long files finish in the background, with progress on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

  3. Take the text with you

    Copy it as plain text, or share it as a .txt file to a messenger, mail or Files. The recording stays next to the transcript for as long as you want.
